# **App Name**: Nexvoro AI

## Core Features:

- AI Role-Specific Mock Interview: Engage in real-time technical interviews tailored to your role and seniority, featuring tool-integrated Gemini logic to ask follow-up questions based on your performance.
- AI Resume Scoring & Analyzer: Upload resumes to receive a detailed ATS score, skill analysis, and missing requirement alerts powered by generative AI.
- Intelligent Performance Feedback: Generate comprehensive post-interview reports with specific technical knowledge, communication, and confidence scores using the Gemini toolset.
- Personalized Career Learning Roadmap: Receive a custom learning plan with recommended resources based on identified weaknesses in your interview performance.
- Persistence & Progress Dashboard: Store all previous interview transcripts, scores, and resume reports securely in the Firestore database.
- Secure Auth & Google Login: Instant user onboarding and profile persistence using Firebase Authentication with Google Login integration.
- Multi-Role Role Selection System: Configure the AI engine to act as a recruiter for over 13 tech roles from UI/UX to Cloud Engineering with experience level tuning.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary Color: Electric Indigo (#5B75FF) to evoke tech innovation and confidence.
- Background Color: Deep Space Midnight (#090A12), providing a premium high-contrast dark theme.
- Accent Color: Orchid Purple (#C09DFF) used in gradients and key buttons to highlight premium AI features.
- A sophisticated font pairing using 'Space Grotesk' for tech-forward headlines and 'Inter' for clear, highly legible body text and reports.
- Clean, thin-line stroke icons with soft gradients to complement the premium SaaS aesthetic.
- A modern glassmorphism design with semi-transparent frosted cards, subtle drop shadows, and high-quality whitespace usage.
- Liquid smooth transitions between interview questions and pulsing indicator glows for active voice-to-text processing.
